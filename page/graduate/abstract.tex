\chapternonum{摘要}

空地协同系统结合了无人机的空中视角与高机动性以及无人车的地面负载与作业能力，在搜索救援、环境探索等任务中具有广阔应用前景。然而在室内、城市峡谷、林下等卫星拒止或卫星信号不可靠环境中，无人机无法依赖卫星定位获得稳定精确的位姿信息，系统必须转而依靠协同感知与相对测距完成定位与导航。与此同时，空地协同通常需要持续的数据交互与测距链路支持；当障碍物造成视线遮挡时，不仅通信质量会下降，还会显著影响基于观测的相对定位精度与协同规划的可靠性。因此，如何在未知复杂环境下实现空地系统的高精度相对定位、主动维持视线连接并生成安全高效的协同轨迹，仍是制约其工程落地的关键问题。为此，本文面向卫星拒止环境，研究了空地异构集群的相对定位架构与面向可见性的协同轨迹规划方法。本文的主要工作如下：

（1）提出了一种基于动态测距基站的空地相对定位框架。针对传统测距定位依赖固定基站、部署成本高且适应性差的问题，本文将测距基站部署在移动无人车上，并以无人车构建的激光建图全局坐标系作为统一参考。通过高频测距时序协议提升测距更新率，并采用误差状态扩展卡尔曼滤波将多边定位解算结果与机载惯性测量数据融合，实现了无人机在无卫星条件下的高频、稳定相对位姿估计。此外，构建了基于三维栅格占据图的共享感知机制，为空地协同规划提供统一的状态与环境输入。

（2）提出了一种基于点云聚类的面向视线维持的空地协同规划方法。针对障碍物遮挡导致的测距/通信链路退化与碰撞风险，本文首先对环境点云进行聚类，并以最小包络椭圆柱对障碍物进行紧凑凸几何近似；在此基础上构造可微的几何避障约束，并设计空地视线连通性代价与地面编队几何约束，将避障安全、视线保持与队形稳定性统一纳入非线性模型预测控制框架。仿真结果表明，该方法在稀疏至中等密度障碍环境中能够实时生成平滑、安全且兼顾视距质量的协同轨迹。

（3）提出了一种基于序列可视空间的两阶段空地协同规划架构。为提升稠密障碍环境下的求解稳定性与鲁棒性，本文构建了“全局一致性规划—局部安全执行”的两阶段架构：全局层基于动力学可行的路径搜索生成引导序列，并在路径上构建时序化的序列可视空间，将复杂的避障与视距约束转化为光滑可导的可见性代价，从而生成满足可见性与队形要求的全局参考；局部层采用多项式轨迹参数化，在时间对齐跟踪全局参考点的同时，结合局部避障力场，实现动力学可行且安全的连续轨迹输出。仿真与实物实验验证了该方法在复杂稠密环境下具有更高的任务成功率与鲁棒性。

\chapternonum{Abstract}

Air–ground cooperative systems leverage the aerial perspective and agility of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) together with the payload capacity and operational endurance of unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs), offering strong potential for search and rescue and environmental exploration. Yet in GNSS-denied or GNSS-degraded settings—such as indoor spaces, urban canyons, and under-canopy environments—UAVs cannot depend on satellite positioning for stable, accurate state estimation; instead, the team must rely on cooperative sensing and relative ranging for localization and navigation. Effective cooperation further requires sustained data exchange and reliable ranging links. When obstacles introduce line-of-sight (LoS) occlusions, communication quality degrades and the observation geometry for relative localization worsens, thereby reducing the reliability of cooperative planning. Consequently, enabling accurate relative localization, proactively maintaining LoS links, and generating safe and efficient cooperative trajectories in unknown, cluttered environments remains a key bottleneck for real-world deployment. To address these challenges, this thesis develops a relative localization architecture and visibility-aware cooperative trajectory planning methods for heterogeneous air–ground teams in GNSS-denied scenarios.

(1) A relative localization method based on dynamic ranging beacons is proposed. To address the high deployment cost and poor adaptability of conventional ranging systems relying on static anchors, ranging beacons are mounted on mobile UGVs, and a LiDAR-mapping global frame built by the UGVs is used as the common reference. A high-rate ranging schedule is designed to increase measurement update frequency, and an error-state extended Kalman filter is employed to fuse multilateration results with onboard inertial measurements, enabling high-frequency and stable relative pose estimation for the UAV without GNSS. In addition, a shared perception mechanism based on a 3D voxel occupancy map is developed to provide unified state and environment inputs for cooperative planning.

(2) A visibility-aware cooperative planning method driven by point-cloud clustering is proposed. To mitigate LoS occlusions that degrade ranging/communication links and increase collision risk, the environment point cloud is clustered and each obstacle is compactly approximated by a minimum-enclosing elliptical cylinder. Based on this representation, differentiable geometric collision-avoidance constraints are constructed, and LoS connectivity costs together with UGV formation geometry costs are formulated. These objectives are integrated into a nonlinear model predictive control framework to jointly account for safety, visibility maintenance, and formation stability. Simulation results demonstrate that, in sparse to moderately cluttered environments, the proposed method can generate smooth and safe cooperative trajectories in real time while improving LoS quality.

(3) A two-stage cooperative planning method based on sequential visible spaces is proposed. To improve robustness and solveability in densely cluttered environments, a two-stage architecture that decouples global consistency planning and local safe execution is developed. The global stage generates a guidance sequence via a dynamically feasible path search and constructs time-ordered sequential visible spaces along the path, converting complex collision and visibility requirements into smooth, differentiable visibility costs to produce global references that satisfy visibility and formation objectives. The local stage uses polynomial trajectory parameterization to track the time-aligned global references while incorporating a local obstacle-repulsion field, producing dynamically feasible and safe continuous trajectories. Both simulations and real-world experiments validate that the proposed two-stage method achieves higher task success rates and stronger robustness in complex, densely cluttered scenarios.

\noindent \textbf{Keywords:} air–ground cooperation; GNSS-denied environments; relative localization; visibility-aware planning
